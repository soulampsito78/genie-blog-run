## Notification and adjudication

Normal complete PASS persists evidence and stays quiet. A meaningful exception produces one concise packet with PRODUCT, RUN_ID, PUBLICATION_DATE, ANOMALY_TYPE, EXACT_PROBLEM, SUPPORTING_EVIDENCE, CUSTOMER_SEND_STATE, RECOMMENDED_RECOVERY and OWNER_ACTION_REQUIRED. Repeated unchanged exceptions stay quiet. A final cohort report states actual completion, missing slots and the remaining transition requirement.

Report KNOWN_ANOMALY_CAUGHT, KNOWN_ANOMALY_MISSED, NORMAL_PASS, NORMAL_FALSE_HOLD and REVIEW_UNAVAILABLE with their real denominators. Separate limited text/image judgements from complete publication PASS. Retain withdrawn false-hold proposals as adjudication evidence. Fifteen slots cannot establish population error rates, and an all-HOLD cohort does not demonstrate normal unattended operation.
